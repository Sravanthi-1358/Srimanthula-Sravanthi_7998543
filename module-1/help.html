Getting Started

The Local Community Event Portal is designed to help you discover, register for, and participate in local community events. Here's how to get the most out of our platform.

Creating an Account

To register for events, simply fill out the registration form with your name, email, and phone number. Your information is kept secure and will only be used for event communications.

Browsing Events

Visit the Events section to see our latest community activities. You can view past event photos in our gallery and learn about upcoming events.

Features Overview

Event Gallery

Browse photos from past community events. Double-click any image to view it in full size. We regularly update our gallery with new event highlights.

Event Registration

Register for upcoming events by selecting your preferred event type and providing your contact information. You'll receive a confirmation via email.

Feedback & Ratings

After attending an event, please share your feedback. Your ratings and comments help us improve future events.

Event Promo Videos

Watch videos showcasing our recent events and community highlights. These videos give you a preview of what to expect.

Location Services

Use our "Find Nearby Events" feature to discover events happening near your location. Your browser will ask for permission to access your location.

Save Your Preferences

Customize your experience by saving your preferred event types. These preferences are stored locally on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Do I need to create an account to register for events?
A: No, you can register for events directly by filling out the registration form. We don't require a separate account creation.
Q: How will I receive event confirmations?
A: Confirmation details will be displayed on the portal immediately after registration. A confirmation email will also be sent to the email address you provided.
Q: Can I change my saved preferences?
A: Yes! Go to the Preferences section to update your preferred event types. You can also clear all saved data if you wish.
Q: How does the location feature work?
A: Click the "Find Nearby Events" button, and with your permission, we'll access your device's GPS to show you nearby events. This helps you discover events close to your location.
Q: Is my personal information secure?
A: Yes, we take privacy seriously. Your information is only used for event-related communications and is never shared with third parties.
Q: What browsers does this portal support?
A: The portal works with all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. For the best experience, please keep your browser updated.
Q: Can I register for multiple events?
A: Yes! You can register for as many events as you'd like. Simply fill out the registration form for each event you're interested in.
Q: How do I provide feedback about my experience?
A: After attending an event, go to the Feedback section and rate your experience. Your feedback is invaluable and helps us improve.

Technical Information

Browser Requirements

  • HTML5 compatible browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
  • JavaScript enabled
  • Cookies or local storage enabled for saving preferences
  • Optional: Geolocation permissions for location-based features

Data Privacy

This portal uses browser local storage to save your preferences. This data is stored only on your device and can be cleared at any time. We do not track or store personal information beyond what you explicitly provide.

Accessibility

We're committed to making this portal accessible to all users. If you experience any accessibility issues, please contact our support team.

Troubleshooting

Location feature not working?

  • Check if your browser has permission to access location
  • Ensure your device's location services are enabled
  • Try refreshing the page
  • Try a different browser

Can't see images in the gallery?

  • Check your internet connection
  • Clear your browser cache and reload
  • Try disabling ad blockers temporarily

Preferences not being saved?

  • Ensure cookies/local storage is enabled in your browser
  • Check if you're in private/incognito mode (data is not saved)
  • Try refreshing the page

Video won't play?

  • Check your internet connection
  • Ensure your browser supports HTML5 video
  • Try a different browser
